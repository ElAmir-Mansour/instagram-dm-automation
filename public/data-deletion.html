Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: May 18, 2026

What Data We Store

AutoResponseBot stores minimal interaction data to prevent duplicate messaging:

  • Your Instagram username
  • The comment ID that triggered the automation
  • The post ID where the comment was made
  • A timestamp of the interaction

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, please contact us with your Instagram username. We will delete all associated records within 30 days.

Automatic Deletion

Interaction logs are automatically considered stale after 24 hours for the purpose of duplicate prevention. Data may be retained for diagnostic purposes but contains no sensitive personal information beyond your public Instagram username.

Contact

For data deletion requests, please open an issue on our GitHub repository or contact the administrator of the Instagram account that triggered the automation.
