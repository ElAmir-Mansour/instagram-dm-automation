Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: May 18, 2026

What Data We Store

AutoResponseBot stores minimal interaction data to prevent duplicate messaging:

  • Your Instagram username
  • The comment ID that triggered the automation
  • The post ID where the comment was made
  • A timestamp of the interaction
  • If you sent a direct message: the text of your messages, the replies sent to you, your Meta-scoped user ID, and the verbatim event we received from Meta

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, please contact us with your Instagram username. We will delete all associated records within 30 days.

Automatic Deletion

Interaction logs are treated as stale after 24 hours for the purpose of duplicate prevention. The verbatim event received from Meta — which, for a direct message, contains the full text of that message — is deleted automatically after 30 days.

Message text and interaction logs are kept beyond that so the account owner can read their own conversation history. This includes the content of private messages. If you want yours removed, use the request route below.

Contact

To request deletion, either:

  • Reply to the Instagram or Facebook account that messaged you and ask for your data to be deleted; or
  • Open an issue on the project issue tracker.

Include the username you used. We will confirm and delete all associated records within 30 days.
